# Product Principles

Every feature should support at least one of these goals.

## Discover

Help users find stories they wouldn't discover elsewhere.

## Personalize

Recommendations should become smarter over time.

## Explain

Recommendations should always explain WHY they were recommended.

## Curate

Quality beats quantity.

Avoid overwhelming users.

## Community

Fans should help fans discover stories.

## Comfort

BLumi should feel warm, cozy, and welcoming.

Never cold or overly technical.
