# BLumi

## Mission

Help every BL fan discover their next favorite story.

---

## Vision

BLumi is a discovery platform dedicated to Boys' Love (BL) series, movies, and anime.

Unlike streaming services, BLumi does not host content.

Instead, BLumi helps fans discover stories through intelligent recommendations, curated collections, personalized experiences, and community-driven discovery.

Our long-term goal is to become the most trusted destination for discovering BL media worldwide.

---

## The Problem

Finding the next BL to watch is harder than it should be.

Existing platforms focus on metadata.

Fans care about emotions.

They search using questions like:

- I want a fluffy romance.
- Give me enemies to lovers.
- I need something like Cherry Magic.
- I want a happy ending.
- I want green flag couples.

Current movie databases don't answer these questions well.

---

## Our Solution

BLumi organizes BL stories around emotions, moods, relationship dynamics, and tropes instead of only genres.

Discovery should feel like getting a recommendation from a friend.

---

## Success

A user finishes a BL series and instinctively opens BLumi to decide what to watch next.
